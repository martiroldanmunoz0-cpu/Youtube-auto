      What If You Fell Into the Grand Canyon? Movies vs Reality #shorts

      Would you ever hike the inner canyon? 👇

      What happens if you fall into the Grand Canyon? Movies vs reality: fall time, heat and rescue cost.
      The canyon is a staircase of ledges, so most falls are far shorter than a mile.
      #grandcanyon #whatif #facts #science #shorts

      Por qué esta idea

      The question names the topic instantly, and 'forget the movies' promises an answer the viewer doesn't know. The myth-vs-reality structure creates a mini payoff every 8-10 seconds, which keeps retention high without falling back on a timeline countdown. The twist is simple, visual and easy to share ('the canyon is a staircase'). It is also true and counterintuitive, so it invites comments like 'I didn't know that' and debate about rescue costs.

      Guion
      1. What if you fell into the Grand Canyon?
      2. Forget the movies. Here's the truth.
      3. Your foot slips.
      4. Movie version: you plunge a full mile, nonstop.
      5. Reality: the canyon is about a mile deep.
      6. You'd hit about one hundred twenty miles per hour.
      7. Movie: you land in a cool, calm river.
      8. Reality: the inner canyon runs twenty degrees hotter than the rim.
      9. At Phantom Ranch, summer days pass 110 degrees.
      10. Movie: a helicopter scoops you up for free.
      11. Reality: rangers run hundreds of rescues every year.
      12. But an air ambulance can cost tens of thousands of dollars.
      13. Now, here's what almost nobody knows.
      14. The canyon isn't one sheer wall. It's a giant staircase of ledges.
      15. You land on a ledge. That's why some survive.
      16. The same canyon that pulls you in is built to catch you.
      17. So step back from that edge, and don't ask yourself…
