      Por qué esta idea

      Guion
      1. Right now, there are usually around ten people orbiting above you, aboard the International Space Station and China's Tiangong station.
      2. They float roughly two hundred fifty miles up, circling the planet every ninety minutes. They would watch every city go dark from above.
      3. And here's the best part. Their capsules are built to bring them home. Soyuz, Dragon, and Shenzhou can all carry crews back to Earth.
      4. Even with mission control silent, those capsules can in principle fly largely on their own, with parachutes doing the final braking.
      5. Meanwhile, down here, you sit under a black sky. Then one night, you notice a bright dot gliding across the stars.
      6. It doesn't blink like an airplane. It moves fast and steady. The space station is one of the brightest objects in the night sky.
      7. Imagine realizing that dot isn't a satellite. It's people. Someone looking back at you. Someone who might be coming home.
      8. So the truth is, the last person on Earth might not be alone for long. Hollywood forgot about everyone looking down.
