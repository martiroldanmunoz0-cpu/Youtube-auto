      #1

      What If You Dug Straight Down? Where You'd Really Come Out #shorts

      pedido-26-01 · pedido

      ⚠️ Frase revisable (71%): «At a hundred feet, groundwater floods in.»

      Did you think it was China? 👇

      What happens if you dig a hole through the Earth from the US? The deepest hole ever dug, the Kola Superdeep Borehole, and where you'd really come out.
      The Kola hole reached about 7.6 miles, only 0.2% of the way to the Earth's center.
      #shorts #whatif #earth #science #facts

      Por qué esta idea

      La pregunta inicial es universal y deja el tema claro en 2 segundos; la estructura por escalas crea una escalada de tensión natural con cifras reales que sorprenden en cada nivel, y el formato mito contra realidad (China) engancha porque casi todos los estadounidenses crecieron oyendo ese mito. El doble giro (Océano Índico + tapa oxidada) da un final que pocos conocen, ideal para comentarios ('I always thought it was China!') y el loop hacia la pala favorece la retención y las repeticiones.

      Guion
      1. What if you dug straight down from Kansas?
      2. Everyone says you'd pop out in China.
      3. Six feet down, you're already exhausted.
      4. At a hundred feet, groundwater floods in.
      5. Two and a half miles down, rock hits 130 degrees.
      6. In 1970, Soviet engineers started drilling Kola.
      7. By 1989, they reached 40,230 feet.
      8. The hole was barely nine inches wide.
      9. Expected heat: 212 degrees. Found: 356.
      10. The rock turned soft as plastic, so they stopped.
      11. That's only zero point two percent of the way down.
      12. Four thousand miles remain, ending in a core hot as the Sun.
      13. You wouldn't reach China. You'd surface in the Indian Ocean.
      14. Today, Kola sits under a rusty metal lid.
      15. Grab your shovel. You've got 7,900 miles to go.
      16. So the next time you pick up a shovel and wonder,…
      Ganchos considerados
      • 9/10 What if you dug straight down from Kansas? (Aclara el tema al instante, es concreto y tiene 8 palabras.)
      • 8/10 Everyone says you'd pop out in China. Wrong. (Mito contra realidad que atrapa a un público estadounidense, pero el tema se entiende un poco menos rápido.)
      • 7/10 How deep could you dig before the Earth stops you? (Plantea un reto claro, pero es menos concreto y tiene 10 palabras.)
      • 7/10 Humans dug seven miles down. Then the rock fought back. (El dato choca, pero el tema no queda tan claro como en una pregunta directa.)
      • 6/10 You grab a shovel. You start digging straight down. (Es inmersivo, pero el tema es menos explícito.)
