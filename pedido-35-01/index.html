      What If You Held a Spoonful of a Neutron Star? #shorts

      pedido-35-01 · pedido

      ⚠️ Dura 30s: menos de los 40 s que quieres

      Would you try to lift it? 👇

      What happens if you hold a teaspoon of neutron star? It weighs about a billion tons, more than every human on Earth combined.
      The star it comes from is only about twelve miles wide, yet it outweighs our Sun.
      #neutronstar #space #whatif #science #shorts

      Por qué esta idea

      It follows the retention rules. The 0 s hook is already a physical impossibility, and the next line is a new fact instead of repeating the premise as a question. It uses dry statement + short punchline ('Combined.', 'It goes through it.'). The viewer lives it in second person. It ends on an exact figure with no 'likely', then cuts so the loop works. One honest flag for the owner: common estimates put Mount Everest at roughly 160 billion tons, while NASA's figure for a sugar cube of neutron star is about 1 billion tons. So 'more than Everest' doesn't hold for a teaspoon, and the opener uses 'every human on Earth, combined' (about 0.5 billion tons), which the data does support. Title suggestion (48 chars): 'What If You Held a Spoonful of a Neutron Star?'
