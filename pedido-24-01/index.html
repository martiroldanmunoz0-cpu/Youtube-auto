      The Man Who Survived Two Atomic Bombs: Tsutomu Yamaguchi #shorts

      pedido-24-01 · caso_real

      ✅ Revisión automática: todo correcto

      Did you know this story? 👇

      The true story of Tsutomu Yamaguchi, the man who survived both atomic bombs, Hiroshima and Nagasaki, in August 1945.
      Around 160 people are believed to have lived through both blasts, but Japan officially recognized only one of them, and not until 2009.
      #history #hiroshima #nagasaki #truestory #shorts

      Por qué esta idea

      La pregunta de probabilidades es irresistible y deja claro el tema al instante. La segunda persona hace que el espectador sienta el destello. Además, se adelanta al espectador: anuncia que la segunda bomba no es lo más loco y le obliga a quedarse hasta el dato poco conocido del reconocimiento oficial en 2009. La escena del jefe incrédulo justo antes del segundo destello es un pico de tensión casi cinematográfico y totalmente documentado.

      Guion
      1. This man survived two atomic bombs.
      2. How did one man survive both atomic bombs, and what do few people know about his story?
      3. Tsutomu Yamaguchi. Twenty-nine-year-old engineer. One business trip to Hiroshima.
      4. August sixth, 1945. You're walking to work with a briefcase.
      5. Two miles away, the first atomic bomb explodes.
      6. The blast bursts your eardrums and blinds you.
      7. Your upper body burns. But you're alive.
      8. You spend the night in a shelter, then board a train home.
      9. Home is Nagasaki. Another bomb is coming.
      10. August ninth. Bandaged, you go back to work.
      11. You tell your boss: one bomb destroyed an entire city.
      12. He thinks you're crazy. Then the window turns white.
      13. Second bomb. Same distance. Same man. Somehow, he survives again.
      14. Around one hundred sixty people likely lived through both.
      15. Japan officially recognized only one: Yamaguchi.
      16. It took until 2009. Sixty-four years later.
      17. He was ninety-three. He died the next year.
      18. So if you ever feel unlucky, remember…
      Ganchos considerados
      • 9/10 This man survived two atomic bombs. (Dato imposible, concreto y de 6 palabras. Se entiende al instante, no revela cómo y abre la curiosidad por saber cómo fue posible.)
      • 8/10 Two atomic bombs. One man. Both survived. (Dato que choca y deja claro el tema, aunque destripa un poco el resultado.)
      • 7/10 What if you survived two atomic bombs? (Pregunta directa que deja claro el tema, pero suena hipotética y no promete una historia real.)
      • 7/10 Could you survive two atomic bombs? (Reto en segunda persona, claro en dos segundos, pero sin el gancho de que es una persona real.)
      • 5/10 You're in Hiroshima. The bomb just fell. (Inmersivo, pero el espectador desconocido no sabe que va a haber una segunda bomba ni cuál es el reto.)
