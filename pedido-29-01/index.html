      The Tunguska event of 1908: what exploded over Siberia and left no crater?
      It flattened about 80 million trees, yet no meteorite was ever found. The answer is an airburst.
      #tunguska #siberia #mystery #space #shorts

      Por qué esta idea

      It repeats what the owner approved: a real story with a protagonist who has a name (as in Yamaguchi), second-person immersion (as in the 'What If…' videos) and a reveal that is solved with logic, not just stated. The detective structure with 3 numbered clues keeps people watching until the last clue, and the upright trees at the center are a visual detail few people know about that 'proves' the airburst. The twist (Chelyabinsk 2013, and the city that could have been destroyed) turns a 1908 story into something relevant today and drives comments. All of the data is well documented: Semenov's account, Kulik's 1927 expedition, the 'telegraph pole' trees, the glowing skies over Europe, and the Chelyabinsk event.
