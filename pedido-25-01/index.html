      What Happens If You Stop Sleeping? The 11-Day Record #shorts

      pedido-25-01 · caso_real

      ⚠️ El sonido desaparece por completo en el segundo 4.1 (audio cortado)

      How long can you go without sleep? 👇

      What happens to your body if you stop sleeping? The true story of Randy Gardner, who stayed awake 264 hours in 1964.
      Guinness stopped tracking sleep-deprivation records because they were too dangerous.
      #sleepdeprivation #whathappensifyoustopsleeping #randygardner #truestory #facts

      Por qué esta idea

      It delivers exactly what the owner asked for (24h, 48h, 72h, hallucinations, Randy Gardner) without feeling like a dry countdown, because every stage is felt in second person and then 'proven' by a real kid with a name. The relatable comparisons (legally drunk, blacking out with your eyes open) are instantly clear to a US viewer. The bizarre detail of him believing he was a Chargers football player is shareable. The double twist rewards viewers who stay to the end: Guinness banning the record and the irony of later insomnia. The closing question loops straight back into the hook and invites comments.

      Guion
      1. This teenager stayed awake for 11 days.
      2. What happens to your body without sleep?
      3. Randy Gardner, seventeen, wanted a science fair project.
      4. Hour twenty-four: you react like a drunk driver.
      5. Your vision blurs, and everything goes dark.
      6. At forty-eight hours, your brain steals microsleeps.
      7. Seconds of blackout, eyes wide open.
      8. Friends Bruce and Joe kept shaking him awake.
      9. By seventy-two hours, your memory slips.
      10. Your heart races. The world looks wrong.
      11. Randy mistook a street sign for a person.
      12. Day four: he believed he was a Chargers player.
      13. Researcher William Dement watched: lucid, barely.
      14. Day eleven, he held a press conference.
      15. Then he slept fourteen hours, and woke up fine.
      16. Guinness stopped tracking sleep records. Too dangerous.
      17. Decades later, Randy battled severe insomnia.
      18. The boy who refused to sleep... couldn't.
      19. And if you ever feel exhausted, remember that…
      Ganchos considerados
      • 9/10 This teenager stayed awake for 11 days. (Concrete shocking fact with a protagonist, and the topic (no sleep) is clear in under 2 seconds.)
      • 8/10 What happens if you stop sleeping completely? (Direct question that names the topic, but with no protagonist or number to anchor it.)
      • 7/10 How long could you stay awake before breaking? (Challenge question that makes the viewer curious, but it is less concrete.)
      • 7/10 You haven't slept in 24 hours. Now what? (Second-person immersion, but it sounds like the middle of the story.)
      • 6/10 Everyone thinks sleep loss just makes you tired. (Contradiction hook, but it is vague and doesn't say what the video will show.)
